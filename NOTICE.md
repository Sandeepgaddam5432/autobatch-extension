## Responsible use

This extension automates the web UIs of third-party platforms (Meta.ai, Google
Labs Flow, Grok, Gemini, ChatGPT, Qwen). It runs in your own logged-in browser
session and downloads only what those pages already show you. You are
responsible for using it in line with each platform's terms of service and
rate limits — the built-in random delays, daily caps and time windows exist
exactly for that. No affiliation with, or endorsement by, any of these
platforms is implied.
